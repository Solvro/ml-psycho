## Project Vision

EchoMind transforms psychology education through immersive, AI-driven conversational training. Our platform empowers students to master active listening techniques in a safe, scalable environment that bridges the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical therapeutic skills.

## What We're Building

EchoMind is an intelligent voice-based training ecosystem that simulates real client interactions using advanced AI technology. Students engage in natural conversations while receiving instant, personalized feedback on their therapeutic communication techniques—from basic paraphrasing to complex emotional mirroring.

### Core Innovation

- **Conversational AI Engine**: Dynamic client personas powered by large language models create authentic, unpredictable dialogue scenarios
- **Real-Time Analysis**: Advanced speech recognition and natural language processing provide immediate feedback on listening techniques
- **Immersive Learning**: Evolution from desktop interactions to full VR therapy simulations with photorealistic avatars
- **Adaptive Learning**: ML-driven personalization adapts scenarios and feedback to individual student progress

## Target Impact

**Primary Users: Psychology Students**

- SWPS University students as our initial cohort
- Expanding across Polish academic institutions
- International scaling potential with multi-language support

**Secondary Users: Educators & Institutions**

- Instructors gain detailed analytics on student progress
- Academic programs can integrate standardized practical training

## Strategic Objectives

### Phase 1: Foundation (MVP Launch)

- **Voice-First Interaction**: Seamless Polish speech recognition and natural conversation flow
- **Intelligent Feedback Engine**: Rule-based evaluation system identifying key active listening techniques
- **Session Analytics**: Comprehensive logging and progress tracking for students and instructors

### Phase 2: Enhancement (Advanced AI)

- **ML-Powered Evaluation**: Custom models trained on therapeutic conversation data
- **Persona Diversity**: Expanded client archetypes covering various backgrounds and communication styles
- **Progress Gamification**: Achievement systems and skill progression tracking

### Phase 3: Immersion (VR Integration)

- **Meta Quest 2 Platform**: Full VR therapy room simulations with realistic avatars
- **Multimodal Feedback**: Visual cues, body language recognition, and spatial audio integration
- **Collaborative Training**: Multi-user scenarios and peer learning experiences

### Phase 4: Ecosystem (Platform Expansion)

- **Multi-Language Support**: International market expansion starting with European universities
- **Integration APIs**: LMS connectivity and third-party educational tool compatibility
- **Research Platform**: Anonymized data insights for therapeutic communication research
